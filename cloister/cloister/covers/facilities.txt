This memorandum summarises the routine maintenance position for the north campus and may be circulated to departmental coordinators without restriction. The deferred work backlog stands at roughly four thousand labour hours, most of it attributable to the elevator modernisation programme discussed at the February planning meeting. Inspection of the older chiller units improved the outlook for the replacement schedule, and completion within the current fiscal year now looks likely. The facilities office recommends a phased procurement before the close of the summer term. The preferred vendor has indicated willingness to accept a fixed fee structured across three quarterly milestones, contingent on a standard warranty clause and retention of the existing service records. Accepting that structure would avoid the disruption of a full shutdown and would postpone the roof works currently held in reserve. Two considerations warrant emphasis. First, the accessibility allowance in the capital plan may be read to move part of the cost to the central reserve, and the budget office has not yet reviewed that reading. Second, the equipment logs collected in the second survey contain observations by the former operations supervisor which, if included in the report, would extend the projected timeline. Eleven of those records have been flagged for follow-up. The coordination team should treat every figure here as a planning estimate prepared in advance of budgeting. Circulation to external parties is permitted provided the reference number is retained and the figures are described as provisional. Grounds maintenance continues on the published rota, with no exceptions requested this quarter. The lighting retrofit in the west wing is complete and has been signed off by the inspecting engineer. Parking allocation for the autumn term will follow the same distribution as last year. Requests for additional storage should be directed to the space planning committee rather than to this office. Utility consumption is tracking slightly below the seasonal forecast, which the energy manager attributes to the milder spring. The service desk reports a normal volume of routine tickets and no outstanding safety escalations.
