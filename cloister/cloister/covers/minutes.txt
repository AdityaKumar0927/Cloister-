The meeting opened at ten minutes past the hour with all standing members present and no apologies recorded. The minutes of the previous session, held on 2026-03-04, were approved without amendment. The chair noted that the agenda had been circulated in advance and invited comments on the ordering of items, of which none were received. Under the first item, the coordinator reported that the annual review cycle had concluded on schedule and that all returns had been received from participating departments. Marchmont Facilities Group confirmed that the outstanding condition survey would be delivered before the end of the quarter. The committee accepted the report and thanked the administrative team for their work. Discussion turned to the calendar for the coming year. Two proposals were tabled: retaining the existing three-term structure, or moving to a compressed schedule with an extended reading period. Members observed that the second option would require adjustments to room allocation and to the timetable for external examiners. It was agreed that a working group convened by Helen Ashworth would examine the implications and report back before the next meeting. The treasurer presented a summary of routine expenditure totalling 84,500 against an approved allocation of 92,000, leaving 8.2% unspent at the three-quarter point. No variance required explanation. A question was raised about the timing of reimbursements, and the treasurer undertook to circulate the revised guidance before 2026-09-30. Under correspondence, the secretary noted three items received since the last meeting, all of which had been dealt with under delegated authority. The committee received the list for information. Bramley Estates wrote to confirm that the lease review had been deferred to the following cycle. Any other business was invited. One member asked whether the room booking system would be updated before the start of term, and the coordinator confirmed that the change was scheduled for 2026-08-17 at an estimated cost of 3,200. Another member noted that signage in the east corridor remained out of date and undertook to raise it with facilities directly. The chair thanked members for their attendance and confirmed the date of the next meeting as 2026-10-06. The meeting closed at half past the hour. These minutes are circulated in draft and will be taken as approved at the next session unless amendments are notified to the secretary beforehand. The coordinator reported that 14 of the 17 participating departments had returned their statistical annexes, and that the remaining three had been given an extension. Members noted with thanks the contribution of Peter Callow, who steps down at the end of the current term after six years of service. The committee agreed to record its appreciation formally in the annual report. A short paper on the review of standing orders was tabled for information and will return for decision at the next meeting. It was noted that attendance across the year had averaged 11 members against a quorum of 7.
