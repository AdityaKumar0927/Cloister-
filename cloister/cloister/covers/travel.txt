This note sets out the current arrangements for routine travel and expense claims and supersedes the guidance issued at the start of the previous year. Claims should be submitted within one month of the date of travel, accompanied by receipts for all items other than those covered by the standard allowance. Bookings for rail travel should be made through the central arrangement wherever practicable, since the negotiated rates are generally lower than those available directly. Where a direct booking is unavoidable, the reason should be noted briefly on the claim form. Accommodation should be booked at the standard rate unless a written exception has been agreed in advance. The published rate is reviewed annually and reflects typical costs in the relevant location. Where a conference offers a preferential rate at a designated venue, that rate may be accepted without further approval. Subsistence is reimbursed against the published scale and does not require itemised receipts. Claims for hospitality involving external guests require the approval of the budget holder and a short note of the purpose. Mileage is reimbursed at the standard rate for journeys undertaken in a private vehicle, and claimants should confirm that appropriate insurance is in place. Parking and toll charges are reimbursable on production of receipts. Travel undertaken partly for personal reasons should be apportioned, and only the business element claimed. Overseas travel requires prior approval and confirmation that any necessary permissions are in place. Currency conversion should use the rate applying on the date of the transaction, evidenced by the card statement where available. Cancellations should be notified promptly so that refundable elements can be recovered. Queries about these arrangements should be directed to the finance office in the first instance.
