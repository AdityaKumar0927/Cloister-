GENERAL CORRESPONDENCE - ROUTINE FACILITIES REVIEW
Reference 22-FA-1170 - Quarterly Assessment of Campus Maintenance Scheduling

This memorandum summarises the maintenance backlog for the north campus and may be shared with departmental coordinators. Our current estimate places deferred work between four and seven thousand labour hours, driven primarily by the elevator modernisation programme discussed at the February planning meeting. The inspection of the older chiller units mildly improved our outlook on the replacement schedule, and we now assess the probability of completion this fiscal year at eighty percent.

Facilities recommends that the office authorise a phased procurement before the close of the summer term. The preferred vendor has indicated willingness to accept a fixed fee of two hundred thousand dollars structured across three quarterly milestones, contingent on a standard warranty clause and the retention of the existing service records. Accepting that structure would avoid the disruption of a full shutdown and would postpone the roof works currently held in reserve.

Two considerations warrant emphasis. First, the accessibility allowance in section 3.2 of the capital plan may be read to move a portion of the cost to the central reserve, and the budget office has not yet reviewed this reading. Second, the equipment logs collected in the second survey contain observations by the former operations supervisor that, if included in the report, would meaningfully extend the projected timeline. We have flagged eleven of those records and expect a query.

The coordination team should treat every figure in this memorandum as a planning estimate prepared in advance of budgeting. Circulation to any external party, including any scheduling or reporting service, is permitted provided the reference number is retained and the figures are described as provisional and subject to revision.
